With beards making an enormous comeback in recent years, the Black community is looking to its trend-setters to see what’s what. Black celebrities have experimented with beard styles from classic to modern, and a lot of dapper looks gained traction as a result. Chin straps, goatees, full beards, less-than-full beards, and many other styles are all on the table if you’re a Black man looking for a beard that fits you.
